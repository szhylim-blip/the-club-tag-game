# Asset Sourcing

Verified while building the current map and VFX plan. Check the license page at
the time assets are downloaded; keep this file updated with the exact pack URL.

## Environment and textures

- **Poly Haven** — CC0 HDRIs, PBR textures and models.
  <https://polyhaven.com/license>
  Use for realistic wood, tile, plaster, metal and lighting.
- **ambientCG** — CC0 PBR materials, HDRIs and simple models.
  <https://docs.ambientcg.com/license>
  Use for concrete, asphalt, metal panels, fabric and modular surface detail.
- **Kenney Modular Buildings** — CC0 modular walls, windows, doors and roofs.
  <https://kenney.nl/assets/modular-buildings>
- **Kenney Furniture Kit** — CC0 furniture and interior props.
  <https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit>
- **Kenney Space Kit** — CC0 modular spaceport and ship pieces.
  <https://kenney-assets.itch.io/space-kit>
- **Quaternius Modular Sci-Fi Megakit** — check the current pack page for its
  exact license; the page offers a Godot source version.
  <https://quaternius.com/packs/modularscifimegakit.html>

## VFX

- **Kenney Particle Pack** — CC0 particle sprites and shader textures.
  <https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack>
- Godot 4 **GPUParticles3D** and shader graphs for gameplay effects; animated
  particle flipbooks can be authored in Blender or EmberGen and imported as
  textures.

## Map direction

- **The House**: Kenney modular building/furniture shells plus Poly Haven and
  ambientCG wood, tile, fabric and plaster materials.
- **Spaceship**: Quaternius sci-fi modules and Kenney Space Kit pieces with
  ambientCG metal panels and emissive strips.
- **The Floor**: Kenney city modules with ambientCG concrete/asphalt surfaces.
- **Lighting**: Poly Haven HDRIs, with lower-resolution versions for gameplay.

## Rules

- Record source URL, author and license for every imported third-party asset.
- Do not redistribute raw asset packs unless the license allows it.
- Do not claim authorship of third-party assets.
- Keep downloaded source archives out of the repository unless their license
  explicitly permits redistribution.
